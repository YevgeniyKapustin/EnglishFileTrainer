How a day goes

The home screen has one button, and it walks you through the day. A session is 20 exercises at most.

  1. 1

    Review

    First, what is due from earlier lessons. The FSRS algorithm picks the day for each exercise, and irregular verbs join in too.

  2. 2

    Continue

    Then the next part of your lesson, in the same order as the book, with the Grammar Bank rules one tap away.

  3. 3

    Practice

    Finished the lesson today? Practice goes round it again, starting with your mistakes. The next lesson opens tomorrow.

A mistake never just disappears: the exercise comes back at the end of the session. It counts as learned after three right answers in a row, on different days.

What you do

Recalling an answer teaches more than recognising it, so everything is typed or said out loud. The number shows how many of each are in the app.

An AI tutor, if you want one

Built in, with no key to paste and no account:

Why?
after a mistake, a short explanation in simple English, based on the rules of the section.
Ask AI
after a right answer, ask anything about the exercise.
Talk
role-play dialogues from the lessons. The AI plays the other person and checks you did the task.
Write
short texts and opinions, marked with up to six corrections.

Offline these buttons are simply hidden and everything else works. Your progress stays on the phone.

See where you slip

Your streak, a calendar of the days you practised, and weak spots: the topics with the most mistakes in the last two weeks. Tap a topic to open its section.
